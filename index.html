The Story

EVERY STREET
HAS A KING.

Crown City runs on power, fear, money, and memory. Downtown belongs to old blood. The East and West are locked in a war that never truly ends. The North profits from leverage. The South collects every debt.

“They called it the City of No Love because mercy did not exist there.”

When the balance breaks, alliances collapse and the entire city is forced to choose a side.
